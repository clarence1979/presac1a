# The failure ladder

Every input tried before the pill, in the order it was tried. Each fixed one thing and broke another. The full page with the clips is `docs/failure-ladder.md`, and the tool `explain_why` with the topic ladder serves it.

## Video input, the alley fight (6 rungs)

| Rung | Input | What came back | What broke |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | clay mannequin video | right camera, right cuts, right alley | bodies fought like game characters |
| 2 | clay video plus a face photo | the right face | the punches still moved like the grey figures |
| 3 | fighters as pencil outlines | real alley, right face | still a mannequin fight: a drawing of a mannequin moving is still a mannequin moving |
| 4 | empty set, camera only | the most natural movement | people in the wrong places |
| 5 | soft grey ghost silhouettes | people in the right places | the original motion was kept, because the silhouette keeps the pose |
| 6 | striped pills with a white dot | real alley, real punches, the same six shots | nothing |

A whole-frame ink video of the fight was refused twice as not safe for work. Plain boxes and a black facing bar were never sent. They were rejected on sight, so treat them as design choices, not measured failures.

## Frame sheets, the jump shot

A sheet has no clock, and it copies the look of whatever it shows.

1. Twelve clay frames: cone pines and a board-game plaza.
2. The same with shadows and a chequered floor: the chequers came back on the floor and columns.
3. The prompt called the sheet a layout diagram: still cones.
4. The set blurred, the pill sharp: the cones went away. This showed that the model copies visible detail.
5. An ink drawing with trees: neat cones and round topiary.
6. An ink drawing with almost no background: real trees and a real plaza. This worked.

## The stand-in after the pill

- A capsule fitted to the body, up to 0.9 m wide, read as a giant dome and loosened the shots. A slim pin replaced it: a small head on a shoulder-width body.
- Legs, boots and hands on the pin were refused: "No limbs." Footprints and a drag scrape as a limbless gait cue were refused too.

## The set

- A detailed clay set came back as cones, balls and plain blocks. The fix is the set-rebuild line and no scenery.
- Mood light, halos and beams in the blockout were refused. The set is coarse and colourless, and the mood comes from the prompt.
- Clay props that fly (an explosion) were copied as clay lobes. The fix was a reference with no debris, then flat coloured blocks named in the prompt.
- A 4-panel place sheet as a second image was copied as a layout and put the person inside the table. Words only is the default, and one panel is an optional mood reference.

## What it teaches

Mark the spot and the facing, and give the model nothing to copy. Then describe the world in words.
