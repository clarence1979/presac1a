# When the method cannot do it

Say it plainly and give the nearest working option. Do not pretend. The MCP tools carry the same list as `scope_notes`.

| The request | The truth | The nearest option |
|---|---|---|
| A locked-off, static shot | The method does not make shots whose camera does not move. The static drafts came out stiff, and the clip is checked for camera motion. | A very slow push in (CAM03), a small slow arc (CAM24) or a gentle rise (CAM22). CAM17 reads as dread, not calm. A locked shot on purpose is UNPROVEN and only worth a try if the person insists. |
| A fight scene | The copyright filter judges the finished video. A night alley brawl was blocked twice, and fight coverage (ACT07) was blocked on every attempt. | Retry once. Then change the setting, clothes and names: the same fight as daytime sparring in sportswear passed. No film or franchise names. |
| 4K of a draft made elsewhere | There is no seed, so a new run is a new take. Tein's docs say a Tein draft can be finalized at 4K. The first provider route ignored the 4K field. | Make the draft on Tein and finalize it (UNPROVEN on every route). Or finalize at 1080p, where the big gain in sharpness is. |
| No photo of the person | The face will not look like anyone in particular. | The camera-only route with the person described in words, or take a clear front-facing photo. |
| More than 30 seconds in one take | One reference stays under 30.00 seconds in total, and one output is 4 to 30 seconds. | Build in parts and extend from the last frame. |
| The same take again | There is no seed. | Finalize the draft you liked, or extend it. |
| Music in the video | Every prompt bans music because it slipped in once. | Add music afterwards in an editor. |
| Exact text or a logo in the picture | The model invents signs and text. | Add text and logos afterwards. |
| Hands, feet, a limp or a stumble in the clip | The pill has no limbs. | Write them in the prompt, in detail. Use a low camera beside the pill for a feet shot. |
| A statue or hero object as a stand-in | A coarse stand-in came back as an odd shape in several templates and was never fixed. | Provide a reference image of the real object. UNPROVEN. |
| More than 3 characters held apart | 7 colours exist, but 3 were proven. | Fewer characters. Beyond 3 is UNPROVEN. |
| Judging voice likeness or lip-sync | A model cannot hear. | The mouth movement and a transcript can be checked. The person must listen. |
| Sending without a yes | Never. | Plan it, show the cost, ask. |
| A tall picture for a phone | Every grey clip is wide (21:9, 1280 by 534). A tall picture has not been tested with this method. | Make the wide version, then crop it to tall in an editor, knowing that a 9:16 crop of the 21:9 clip keeps only about 24 percent of the width, so the sides are lost and the subject can be cut off. Keep the subject in the middle quarter of the frame. |
