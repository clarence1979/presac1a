3.4 Real hardware and safety

Electric and Electronic, SENSE FORGE, Operation Sentry and Drone Blocks Studio teach skills used with real electronics and drones. The Apps themselves are simulations; real equipment carries real risks.

  • Follow the manufacturer's instructions, your school's risk assessment and adult supervision whenever you use real equipment.
  • Never work on mains power wiring. The projects are for low-voltage, battery or USB-powered equipment only.
  • Take care with soldering irons, lithium batteries, motors and moving parts. Never aim any launcher or projectile at people or animals.
  • Fly drones only where it is legal and safe, and follow your country's aviation rules (the Civil Aviation Safety Authority in Australia, the Federal Aviation Administration in the United States).
  • The electrical safety content is general education. It is not electrical training or a qualification, and it is written for Australian conditions; rules differ between countries.

3.5 Ethical hacking

Everything in Hacking Central and Thornwick is simulated: no real system, network or person is attacked. The names, websites, people and phishing pages in them are made up.

Use what you learn only on systems you own or have written permission to test. Accessing or interfering with a computer system without permission is a crime in Australia (Criminal Code Act 1995 (Cth), Part 10.7), in the United States (Computer Fraud and Abuse Act) and in most other countries. Do not use prompt-injection techniques against real AI services in breach of their rules.
