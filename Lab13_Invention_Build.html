Lab 13: Build an invention that works

Pick one of eight inventions — six use the Sentry lab Grove kit on the Pi 5, two use just a camera and AI on a Windows laptop (or the Pi) — or describe your own idea and an AI writes your build guide and draws your finished invention. You get 6 lessons. Each lesson has one job and a checkpoint. Follow the tabs in order.

How to use this page

  1. It saves itself on this computer every time you type (look at the top right).
  2. At the end of every lesson press Save .json and keep the file on your OneDrive or a USB stick. School computers can be wiped.
  3. On another computer, open this page and press Load .json to carry on.
  4. In Lesson 6 press Export PDF and upload that PDF to SEQTA. Every team member uploads it.
  5. Stuck? Read the Next to do box, then ask your team, then ask .

Five rules

  1. Pick one project in Lesson 1 and stick to it. Change it only if agrees.
  2. Every lesson ends with a checkpoint. Show ; they initial it.
  3. Change one thing, run, check. Never change three things at once.
  4. Film it the moment it works. Hardware breaks at the worst time.
  5. Safety: Pi OFF when you plug parts in. Foam rockets only, never at a face. Your AI key stays secret.

How your 13 marks work

The same marks for every project — one of the six, or your own idea. You get marks for what you build, change, test and explain. Using the AI to write a guide earns nothing by itself. The Evidence column checks this page for you; decides the mark.
