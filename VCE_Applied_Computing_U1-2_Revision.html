How to run a session

  1. Pick a session tab. Each tab is a complete 45-minute lesson: run sheet → teach → response clinic → student practice → summary PDF.
  2. Press “Start session” in the timer bar (or “Start session & section 1” on the run sheet). The 45:00 clock counts down and the first section timer starts. Sections auto-advance with a chime; the session clock chimes at 5 minutes, 1 minute and time-up, and the screen flashes red when the 45 minutes are gone.
  3. Press “Present” to teach from full-screen slides (arrow keys or space to move, Esc to exit). Both clocks stay visible in the top-right of the presenter view.
  4. Response clinic: in presenter mode each clinic slide shows the weak answer first — have students mark it, press Enter to reveal why it loses marks, then Enter again for the strong response. The commentary paraphrases patterns from VCAA Applied Computing examination reports.
  5. Practice: students work through the auto-marked set on their own devices (open the same file), or you run it on the projector. “Mark all” gives a score; short answers are marked on key ideas with a model answer.
  6. Summary PDF: every session has a one-click PDF of the key terms, memory hooks and exam tips — generated inside the page, no internet needed.

Scope note. Units 1 and 2 are school-assessed, so there is no VCAA exam for them. Content is mapped to the Units 1 & 2 key knowledge of the VCE Applied Computing Study Design (accredited from 2025). Sample questions are original and written in VCAA style; where a question is “modelled on” a VCAA Units 3 & 4 exam question it is because that topic is common to both levels and the examiner commentary transfers directly. Examiner commentary is drawn from the VCAA Software Development examination reports 2020–2025 and Data Analytics reports 2020–2021.
